# Electronic contract production gates
Preview only: browser-memory synthetic identity, illustrative quote, consent, SHA-256 digest, browser print/PDF. Digest is not an electronic signature or proof of identity. No server persistence.
Before production: confirm actual property title, exact unit, permitted use, tax registration suitability, operator registration and reviewed lease terms (deposit, VAT, permitted use, mail, cancellation/refund, renewal, disputes). Choose identity verification provider and electronic signature provider, validate representative authority for corporate tenants, store versioned canonical documents and provider evidence in private storage, use server timestamps and authenticated access, retention/deletion policies, consent versioning and audit events. Generate server-side PDF and bind payment to signed contract hash and approved quote. Test cross-tenant access, expired identity, replay, changed terms, failed payments and refunds. Do not enable live routes until approved.
